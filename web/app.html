People

Per-sender rules and context. VIP: always drafted, top of the queue, instant notification. Always draft: beats the bulk-mail filters. Auto-skip: never drafted. Notes feed the drafting context.

Rehearsal

Pick replies you have already sent. MailPilot drafts its own reply to the same email, and you read the two side by side. Nothing is sent, and nothing is queued — this is a mirror, not an outbox.

Load your Sent folder to choose messages. Only replies are listed — a rehearsal needs the email you were answering.
